🏥  MediLink AI 
Bridging the “Incomplete History” Gap and making visits time-
efficient
MediLink AI is a patient-controlled digital health passport that converts
scattered medical records into structured medical timelines and AI
generated summaries powered by a RAG-based system, while every
access and modification is cryptographically audited through blockchain
with verified doctor identities.
🚨 THE PROBLEM 
Healthcare decisions often rely on fragmented and subjective patient
history, which leads to inefficiency and medical risk.
1) Patient Bias
Patients often carry only the records they think are relevant to a specific
visit, causing doctors to miss critical background information.
2) Memory & Communication
History-taking is hindered by patients forgetting details, being unable to
relay their entire history in one sitting, or dismissing symptoms due to
introversion.
3) Data Isolation
Hospitals operate on siloed systems, leaving doctors without access to past
diagnoses, medications, or surgeries from other facilities.
4) Time consumption and Safety Risks

💡  THE SOLUTION 
The Secure AI Health Passport
MediLink AI creates a portable AI-powered medical identity that organizes a
patientʼs entire medical history into a structured, verifiable, and secure

🧠  AI Medical Summary & Intelligence Engine (RAG and ML-
Based)
MediLink AI uses a Retrieval-Augmented Generation RAG) system to analyze
patient medical records, ML models to recognise  and generate structured
medical insights grounded in real documents.
The AI retrieves relevant information from uploaded reports before generating
responses, which helps reduce hallucinations and ensures answers are tied to
actual medical data.
The system provides:
AI-generated medical summaries based on patient records
Chronological diagnoses and medication timelines
Allergy and risk factor identification
Clinical pattern recognition across reports
Doctors can also interact with the AI through natural language chat, asking
questions about the patientʼs history.
All AI responses / summaries include references to the specific reports, lab
results, or research sources used, ensuring transparency and reliability.
